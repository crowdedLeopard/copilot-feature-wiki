## What's changing

The Sales Qualification Agent in Dynamics 365 Sales can now follow written personalisation guidance when it generates outreach emails. In the agent settings, an administrator can describe the outreach scenario, the call-to-action, subject line guidance, tone and voice, brand-language guardrails (approved phrases and banned words), message length and formatting preferences. Learn lists these seven areas, and the roadmap item describes the same list.

The guidance is optional. Learn states that all email personalisation settings are optional and that, if nothing is configured, the agent continues to generate outreach emails using the default instructions. Administrators can start from predefined example scenarios (such as event follow-up or trial ending outreach), edit one into a custom scenario, or use personalisation tokens drawn from the lead record, the agent's account research and the agent settings. Learn says only one custom outreach scenario can be saved and up to 3 custom `Lead` table fields can be added as tokens.

The roadmap item is marked Launched, with a preview target of April CY2026 and a general availability target of July CY2026, for Worldwide (Standard Multi-Tenant) on the web. The Learn article is still headed as a preview feature and as prerelease documentation subject to change at the time of writing (November 2026), so treat its limits as the preview's.

## Why it matters

This moves the wording of outbound sales email from the agent's defaults to rules your sales and brand teams write. If nobody acts, the agent keeps using its default instructions, which Learn describes as designed for cold outreach emails. The change therefore matters most to tenants that already run the agent in Research and engage mode, where the agent emails leads.

Two things follow for a service owner. Guidance is free text, so it can carry commercial claims; the page's own example tells the agent to quote different discounts by industry. And AI-written emails are billed on consumption, so a more elaborate template of instructions is a decision about brand risk and spend, not only style.

## Risk assessment

**Data exposure**

- Guidance can include personalisation tokens from the lead record (such as first name, company name, industry, city and country), the agent's account research and the agent settings. Custom tokens can add up to 3 text fields from the `Lead` table, so review which fields you expose to the email text.

**Security & identity**

- The documented controls are settings inside the Sales Qualification Agent. Learn names admin permissions in Dynamics 365 Sales as a prerequisite for setting the agent up; it does not describe a separate permission for the email guidance field.

**Autonomy & actions**

- In Research and engage mode the agent drafts outreach emails to leads. Instructions such as discount offers, banned words and length limits steer that output. For message length and formatting, Learn says the agent uses them as targets rather than rigid constraints, so do not rely on them as enforcement.

**Change & support**

- Saving an edit to a selected example scenario turns it into the single custom scenario, and editing another example overwrites the earlier custom text. Keep a copy outside the settings page.
- Test changes on a small scale before production, as the agent's setup article advises.

## Cost and licensing

Learn states that selected Copilot and agent capabilities in Dynamics 365 Sales use consumption-based billing, charging per use, and that these capabilities use Microsoft Copilot Studio messages as the billing unit. Two billing models are supported, prepaid capacity (Copilot Studio message packs) and pay-as-you-go. The Sales Qualification Agent overview adds that the agent uses the capacity assigned to your tenant. Both models require the Dynamics 365 Sales environment to be linked to a Power Platform environment.

Setting up the Sales Qualification Agent lists a Copilot Studio licence among its prerequisites.

On the email settings page, choosing a fixed email template means the agent does not use generative AI or the configured knowledge sources to draft the content, and no Copilot credits are consumed for emails generated that way. The AI personalisation option is the generative route. Microsoft Learn publishes no rate or message count specific to personalised outreach emails at the time of writing (November 2026), and its pages use both "messages" and "Copilot credits" for the meter.

## Scope

The guidance applies to outreach emails from the Sales Qualification Agent, which has a Research mode (selected by default and not editable) and an optional Engage capability. The email personalisation page is documented under the agent's email instructions, and the agent setup article describes customised email instructions as an additional setting for Research and engage mode.

The roadmap record lists Worldwide (Standard Multi-Tenant) and the web platform; no other clouds are listed on the record. Prerequisites in the setup article include admin permissions in Dynamics 365 Sales and server-side synchronisation with Exchange: without it, the agent does not generate outreach emails or engage with leads. The roadmap states the feature is enabled by admins, makers or marketers for end users.

## Governance guidance

### Before rollout

- Decide which tone, banned words and call-to-action your sales and brand owners will approve, and who owns the text in the **Email guidance** field.
- Check whether you want AI-written emails at all: compare the AI personalisation option with the fixed Email template option in the controls table.
- Confirm the billing model for the agent (prepaid capacity or pay-as-you-go) and the linked Power Platform environment before more emails are generated.

### At rollout

- Start from one predefined example scenario, edit it and save it as the single custom scenario, keeping a copy of the text elsewhere.
- Add only the `Lead` table fields you intend to appear in emails as custom personalisation tokens (maximum 3).
- Avoid putting commercial offers such as discounts in the guidance unless sales and legal have approved them.

### After rollout

- Read a sample of generated emails against the banned words, length and formatting rules, as Learn calls those preferences targets.
- Review consumption against the capacity you assigned and revisit the guidance if spend rises.
