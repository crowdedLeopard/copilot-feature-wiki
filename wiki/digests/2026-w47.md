## This week in one paragraph

64 briefings changed across 5 topics: 16 in Copilot Studio & agents, 27 in Power Platform, 11 in Governance & security, 8 in Microsoft 365 Copilot, 2 in Modern Work. 5 carry an "Act now" rating because they are on by default or close to a deadline. 12 are rated high risk, mostly because they move organisational data or let agents act on their own.

## Act now
