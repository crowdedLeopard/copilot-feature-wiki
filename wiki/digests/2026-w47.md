## This week in one paragraph

41 briefings changed across 5 topics: 9 in Copilot Studio & agents, 12 in Power Platform, 5 in Governance & security, 3 in Microsoft 365 Copilot, 12 in Modern Work. 6 carry an "Act now" rating because they are on by default or close to a deadline. 5 are rated high risk, mostly because they move organisational data or let agents act on their own.

## Act now

- **[Power Pages: security agent in design studio configures authentication and authorisation](/features/power-pages-design-studio-configure-authentication-with-security-agent/)** (Medium risk). An AI chat panel in the Power Pages design studio Security workspace reviews and configures authentication, web roles and table permissions, applying changes only after the maker approves them.

Teams will store an AI-generated .meeting memory file for recapped meetings so Copilot and Facilitator can answer questions after the transcript expires. On by default, kept five years unless admins or Purview say otherwise.

Copilot-licensed organisers can get an AI summary of a scheduled meeting without retaining a transcript or recording. The summary is a read-only Word file in the organiser's OneDrive that expires after 120 days by default.

Leaders and delegates will be able to pick My Team, a saved audience or a custom one when they post a Storyline Announcement. Targeted for January CY2027; Microsoft says no admin action is required.
- **[Work IQ: business skills for agents through Work IQ MCP](/features/work-iq-business-skills-to-manage-your-business-processes-in-work-iq-mcp/)** (Medium risk). Business skills are natural-language process instructions stored in Dataverse that agents discover through Work IQ MCP to follow organisational processes.
- **[Work IQ: real-time Teams meeting transcripts for apps and agents](/features/work-iq-real-time-transcripts/)** (High risk). Apps and agents can receive Teams meeting transcripts while the meeting is in progress through Work IQ. Admins control it with a separate Teams setting that is off by default.
